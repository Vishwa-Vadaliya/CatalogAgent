# Jaeger-LeCoultre - model names not in the master list: confidence report

Scope: the 150 ModelName values on clean refs of the `Jaeger-LeCoultre final refs` tab (Length 2-3 plain numbers excluded) that are not among the 51 JLC names in the master workbook. Covers 781 refs. Nothing in the sheet was changed.

## How to read this

- **Confidence** = how sure we are the exact string is a valid, real JLC model/collection name (0-100). High >= 80, Medium 50-79, Low < 50. Based on web research (dealer, auction, database, wiki pages), not on JLC's own catalogue.
- **On official site**: jaeger-lecoultre.com returned 403 to every direct fetch, and site: searches mostly returned only current collections. **`yes`** means a jaeger-lecoultre.com (or press.jaeger-lecoultre.com) page naming the model was found. **`unknown`** means it could not be checked, NOT that it is absent. Most discontinued models cannot be confirmed this way.
- **Suggested canonical** = the one name to keep; names sharing a canonical are the near-duplicate groups.
