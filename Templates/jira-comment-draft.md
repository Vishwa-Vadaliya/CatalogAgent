# Jira comment draft

**Never post this.** Put it in the chat reply for the user to paste.

```
<What was done, in one or two lines.>

Scripts:
- <path under Output/Queries/...> — <what it does>

How to run:
- <which steps, in what order; what to paste back>

- <the numbers or conclusions, if the task produced any>

- <what is needed from whom, or "nothing — ready to review">
```

Keep it factual. If a step failed or was skipped, say so here rather than leaving the reader to
discover it. Don't claim anything was verified that wasn't.
